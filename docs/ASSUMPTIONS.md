# Assumptions and open questions

Every decision made where the brief is silent, plus the few questions that genuinely need the founder's answer.
Assumptions are numbered `A-nnn` and referenced from ADRs, the domain model and the roadmap.
When the founder answers a question, the answer is recorded here and the assumption is marked **confirmed** or **changed**.

Status legend: `proposed` (my call, proceeding), `confirmed` (founder agreed), `changed` (founder overruled; see note).

**2026-09-22: the founder approved the blueprint without answering Q1–Q8, so every default below applies and is marked `confirmed`.** Questions stay listed so answers can still change them later (mark `changed` with a note).

## 2. Product and market assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Status |
|----|------------|--------|
| A-001 | **Launch definition.** "First paying customers" are trading and distribution companies (1–50 users, 1–5 legal entities, 1–20 branches/warehouses) in Iraq and the GCC. At launch they must run: chart of accounts and GL with IFRS statements; customers, suppliers, items; quotes to cash and requisitions to payment with partial deliveries and invoicing; multi-warehouse stock with FIFO/average costing, lots and serials; bilingual (EN/AR) invoices and statements; bank reconciliation and post-dated cheques; multi-currency with revaluation; roles, approvals and audit; report builder with drill-down; import of opening balances. That is milestones M1–M7 plus the import toolkit, custom fields and workflow items of M8 (see ROADMAP.md "Launch gate"). | confirmed |
| A-002 | **Deployment: both.** Cloud SaaS multi-tenant is the primary product; on-premise is the same container image run single-tenant. One codebase, one schema, no feature divergence. | confirmed |
| A-003 | **IFRS by default.** Default chart of accounts template, revenue recognition point, FX treatment (IAS 21), inventory (IAS 2: lower of cost and NRV, FIFO or weighted average; LIFO not offered), fixed assets (IAS 16) and leases (out of v1) follow IFRS for SMEs where simpler and full IFRS otherwise. Local statutory charts (Iraq Unified Accounting System, Saudi/GCC layouts) are mappings over the IFRS chart, with parallel adjustment books when required. | confirmed |
| A-004 | **Tax regimes seeded** for Iraq (no general VAT; sales tax on specified goods and services; contractor withholding), Saudi Arabia (15% VAT, ZATCA e-invoicing phase 2), UAE (5% VAT), Bahrain (10% VAT), Oman (5% VAT), Qatar and Kuwait (no VAT), Jordan (16% GST), Egypt (14% VAT with e-invoicing), Turkey (20% VAT) and a generic "EU VAT" template. Rates and rules are configuration, not code, and each template is validated by a local accountant before a customer goes live in that country. | confirmed |
| A-005 | **Working week** defaults to Sunday–Thursday for Iraq and GCC companies, Monday–Friday elsewhere; configurable per company and used for due dates, escalations and schedules. Public-holiday calendars are per company. | confirmed |
| A-006 | **Calendars.** Gregorian is the system calendar for all posting. Hijri (Umm al-Qura) is available for display and printing where a company enables it. Fiscal years may start in any month; a 13th adjustment period is supported. | confirmed |
| A-007 | **Languages.** UI in English and Arabic at launch, with full RTL. Master data fields that print on documents (item names, account names, partner names, UoM, payment terms, addresses) are bilingual, with automatic fallback to the other language. The i18n framework supports adding Kurdish (Sorani, RTL), Turkish and French later without schema change. | confirmed |
| A-008 | **Currencies.** Full ISO 4217 list seeded (over 160 codes) with minor units; company-level overrides for practical precision and cash rounding (for example IQD rounded to 250 or 1,000 dinars on cash documents, KWD/BHD/OMR at 3 decimals). | confirmed |
| A-009 | **Point of sale, manufacturing/MRP, HR/payroll and e-commerce connectors** are out of v1 as stated, but the model keeps hooks: the stock ledger accepts a `production` entry family, partners have an `employee` role, and sales orders carry a `channel` attribute. | confirmed |
| A-010 | **Not built in v1** and not pretended: payroll, project accounting beyond a project dimension, lease accounting (IFRS 16), hedge accounting, tax filing submissions (only reports and export files), electronic payment file generation (SWIFT MT101/pain.001) is M8 stretch, EDI. Each is listed in ROADMAP.md "Deferred". | confirmed |

## 3. Architecture and stack assumptions (see ADRs for reasoning)

| ID | Assumption | Status |
|----|------------|--------|
| A-020 | Backend in **C# on .NET 10 LTS**; PostgreSQL 17; front end in **TypeScript + React 19**. Reasoning in ADR-0002 and ADR-0013. | confirmed |
| A-021 | **Monorepo** with the .NET solution, the web app and infrastructure in one repository, one CI pipeline. | confirmed |
| A-022 | **Multi-tenancy:** shared database and shared schema with a `tenant_id` column on every tenant-scoped table and PostgreSQL row-level security, plus an optional dedicated-database tier for large or regulated tenants using the identical schema. On-premise is a dedicated database with one tenant. ADR-0004. | confirmed |
| A-023 | Infrastructure kept deliberately small: PostgreSQL, S3-compatible object storage, the API/worker containers and headless Chromium for PDF. **No Redis, no message broker** in v1; queues and the outbox live in PostgreSQL. A columnar store (ClickHouse) is introduced only if the M7 benchmark on PostgreSQL read models misses the 10M-line target. ADR-0021. | confirmed |
| A-024 | **Identifiers** are UUIDv7 everywhere; human-readable document numbers come from numbering series. Timestamps are `timestamptz` in UTC; posting dates are calendar dates in the company's time zone. ADR-0011. | confirmed |
| A-025 | **Precision.** Monetary amounts stored as `numeric(20,6)` and rounded to the currency's minor unit only when posted or printed; unit prices and unit costs `numeric(24,10)`; quantities `numeric(24,9)` in the item's base unit; exchange rates `numeric(24,12)`. Rounding: half away from zero by default, configurable per company (half even available). ADR-0005. | confirmed |
| A-026 | **Revenue recognition point** defaults to the invoice (the tax point in every target regime), with COGS recognised at shipment. A period-close checklist step accrues shipped-not-invoiced revenue (auto-reversing). Companies may switch the point to shipment. ADR-0026, POSTING_RULES. | confirmed |
| A-027 | **FX revaluation** of open AR/AP items posts unrealised gain/loss with automatic reversal on the first day of the next period (as the brief requires). Foreign-currency bank and cash balances are translated permanently (no reversal) because their carrying amount genuinely changes; this is configurable per company. ADR-0017. | confirmed |
| A-028 | **Reversals** post a mirrored journal with debits and credits swapped (never negative amounts), flagged `is_reversal` and linked to the original, so turnover reports can net or show both. Posting into a closed period is refused; corrections post in the first open period with a reference to the original. ADR-0026. | confirmed |
| A-029 | **Costing granularity.** Cost is tracked per company and item; a company may choose to value per warehouse instead. FIFO layers are per (company, item, warehouse, lot/serial). Moving average uses a daily average-cost period so backdated entries recompute deterministically. Standard cost variances post at receipt. Cost adjustments run synchronously after each posting; if more than 2,000 downstream entries must be re-applied, the run continues as an immediate background job with the item marked "valuation pending" (target completion: seconds). ADR-0008. | confirmed |
| A-030 | **Negative stock** is blocked by default. Companies may allow it per warehouse or item category, in which case issues are costed at the expected (last/standard) cost and adjusted when the receipt arrives. ADR-0008. | confirmed |
| A-031 | **Numbering.** Gapless series are allocated inside the posting transaction under a row lock; drafts carry a temporary draft number. Non-gapless series may allocate at creation. Per company, branch, fiscal year and document type. ADR-0016. | confirmed |
| A-032 | **API** is REST/JSON with OpenAPI 3.1, URL versioning (`/api/v1`), cursor pagination, `Idempotency-Key` on all mutating calls, ETags for optimistic concurrency, HMAC-signed webhooks. GraphQL is not offered; the semantic-layer query endpoint covers ad-hoc analytics. ADR-0012. | confirmed |
| A-033 | **Front-end grid and pivot** are built in-house on TanStack Table/Virtual rather than a commercial grid, to keep RTL, accessibility, licensing and drill-down fully under our control. ADR-0013. | confirmed |
| A-034 | **Authentication** is built-in (email + password with Argon2id, TOTP and WebAuthn passkeys for MFA) plus OIDC federation. Sessions use short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens. ADR-0014. | confirmed |
| A-035 | **Audit log** is an append-only table with a per-tenant hash chain; the chain head is anchored daily to object storage with object-lock. ADR-0015. | confirmed |
| A-036 | **Custom fields** are stored in a JSONB column on every extensible entity, described by metadata that drives validation, UI, API and reporting. ADR-0019. | confirmed |
| A-037 | **PDF and print** are HTML/CSS templates (Scriban/Liquid syntax) rendered by headless Chromium; this is the only approach that handles Arabic shaping, bidi and fonts reliably. ADR-0022. | confirmed |
| A-038 | **Global search** uses PostgreSQL full-text and trigram indexes per tenant; an external search engine is not introduced unless measured. ADR-0023. | confirmed |
| A-039 | **Email** goes through an SMTP/API provider abstraction (any provider); SMS/WhatsApp notifications are a later adapter. | confirmed |
| A-040 | **AI provider** is abstracted; the reference implementation uses the Claude API. AI never executes raw SQL; it produces semantic-layer queries executed under the user's permissions, and it can only create drafts. ADR-0028. | confirmed |
| A-041 | **Backups**: point-in-time recovery with 35-day retention for SaaS; documented restore drill in M10. On-premise ships the same scripts. | confirmed |
| A-042 | **Licensing** of the codebase is proprietary (all rights reserved) until the founder decides otherwise; only permissively licensed dependencies (MIT/Apache/BSD/PostgreSQL) are used, checked in CI. | confirmed |
| A-044 | **Segregation of duties and super users.** The all-access grant `*` (owner, admin) is excluded from SoD conflict evaluation; members holding it are listed as super users in the SoD report instead. Every other grant, including module wildcards, is evaluated. | confirmed |
| A-045 | **Security bookkeeping survives refusals.** A request that ends in an error normally rolls back its unit of work; failed-login counters, lockouts, refresh-token reuse revocation and the audit events of refusals are committed anyway through an explicit `CommitOnFailure` flag set by the identity services. | confirmed |
| A-046 | **HTTP status comes from the error kind.** Domain errors carry a kind (validation 422, not found 404, conflict 409, forbidden 403, unauthorized 401, locked 423, rate limited 429); codes are stable strings and never parsed for status. Cross-tenant access always answers 404. | confirmed |
| A-047 | **API keys** are `qk_<tenant id>.<secret>`; only the SHA-256 of the secret is stored; a key acts as its creating member with optionally narrowed scopes and never as an owner. | confirmed |
| A-048 | **Emails are stored lower-cased** and compared exactly; sign-in is case-insensitive by construction. | confirmed |
| A-049 | **Default security policy**: 12-character minimum passwords, lockout after 10 failures for 15 minutes, 10-minute access tokens, 14-day sessions, 5-minute step-up window, MFA optional (tenant admins can require it). | confirmed |
| A-050 | **Two audit chains.** Every tenant has its own hash chain (`app.aud_events`); events recorded by a request that never enters a tenant (failed sign-in before tenant selection, password reset) go to one platform chain in `control`, readable by platform operators only. A request that starts anonymous and then enters a tenant (sign-up, invitation acceptance, SSO) carries its earlier events into that tenant, attributed to the person it ended up as. ADR-0015. | confirmed |
| A-051 | **Audit events are written at commit.** The sink buffers a unit of work's events and inserts them immediately before the transaction commits, so the per-tenant chain lock is held for microseconds instead of for the whole request; a rolled-back request leaves no events (refusals that must be kept use `CommitOnFailure`, A-045). | confirmed |
| A-052 | **Explicit beats captured.** EF change capture records every insert, update and delete of an audited entity; when a service also records an explicit event for the same record in the same unit of work (posted, revoked, invited), the explicit event is kept and inherits the captured before/after/diff, and the captured row event is dropped. Updates store only the changed fields in `before`/`after` plus a field-level `diff`; creates and deletes store the full snapshot. | confirmed |
| A-053 | **Hashing happens in the database, verification outside it.** The canonical text of an event is rendered by PostgreSQL (`app.aud_canonical`) and the chaining trigger computes `hash = SHA-256(prev_hash ‖ canonical)` under the tenant's head lock; the application role cannot set hashes or touch chain heads. The verifier recomputes every link in application code from the same rendering and compares the head and the newest anchor. A party with schema-owner rights who also rewrites the hashing function is outside the chain's threat model and is caught by anchors. | confirmed |
| A-054 | **Anchor store.** Chain heads are anchored to an append-only, hash-linked JSON-lines file (`Quicker:Audit:Anchoring`), the on-premise default; the object-lock store arrives with the storage client in M1.8 and the daily schedule with the scheduler in M1.8. Until then anchoring and verification run on demand (API) and through the platform-wide job entry points. | confirmed |
| A-055 | **Secrets never enter the log.** Entity properties marked redacted, and any payload property whose name denotes a secret (password, secret, token, hash, key), are replaced by `[redacted]`; a change to a redacted field is still visible in the diff. Login counters and last-login stamps are ignored by capture because sign-in has its own events. | confirmed |
| A-043 | **Performance budgets**: p95 under 300 ms for everyday API calls with 50 concurrent users per tenant on a 4-vCPU database; interactive pivots over 10M fact rows under 5 s; document posting under 500 ms p95 including cost adjustment for typical chains. Verified in M10 with seeded data. | confirmed |

## 4. Process assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Status |
|----|------------|--------|
| A-060 | The founder reviews at the end of each milestone; within a milestone I proceed on documented defaults and stop only for expensive-to-reverse decisions. | confirmed |
| A-061 | Every session starts by reading `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/PROGRESS.md` and the ADR index, and ends by updating `docs/PROGRESS.md` and committing. | confirmed |
| A-062 | Demo data is generated by a deterministic seeder (fixed random seed) so demos and performance tests are reproducible. | confirmed |
| A-063 | Documentation language is English; UI strings are authored in English and translated to Arabic in the same pull request that adds them (no untranslated strings reach `main`). | confirmed |
| A-064 | **ISO currencies are platform reference data** (`control.currencies`, seeded by the migrator, read-only for the application). Tenants enable currencies per company (`org_company_currencies`) with their own display precision and cash rounding increment; the functional currency is always enabled and is immutable once the company exists. | proposed |
| A-065 | **Fiscal year codes**: `FY2026` for a calendar year, `FY2026/27` for a year spanning two calendar years. Creating a company opens the fiscal year that contains today (in the company's time zone) when its calendar has none; later years are opened explicitly. The 13th (adjustment) period shares the last day of the year and is never selected by posting date. | proposed |
| A-066 | **Period states** are stored only where set; a period without a row is `open` in an open year, `never_opened` in a future year and `hard_closed` in a closed year. Ordinary changes (open ↔ soft_closed → hard_closed) need `accounting.period.manage`; leaving `hard_closed` needs `accounting.period.reopen`, a reason and recent authentication, and is audited as an override. | proposed |
| A-067 | **Exchange-rate resolution order**: identity, direct rate, inverse of the direct rate, cross rate through the company's functional currency (both legs direct or inverse). Correcting or deleting a stored rate requires a reason. Provider imports never overwrite a rate entered manually on the same date. | proposed |
| A-068 | **Tenant defaults** created at sign-up: fiscal calendar `standard` (January, 12 periods), business calendars `sun_thu` (default) and `mon_fri`, system dimensions `BRANCH`, `COST_CENTER` (hierarchical), `DEPARTMENT` (hierarchical), `PROJECT`, rate types `spot`, `closing`, `average`, `budget` (system) plus `official` and `market`, and the units PCS, DZ, CTN, PLT, KG, G, TON, L, ML, M, CM, M2, HR, DAY with their conversions. A branch's dimension value is coded `<COMPANY>-<BRANCH>`. | proposed |
| A-069 | **Global unit conversions** are defined once per pair within a family and resolved directly, inversely, or through one intermediate unit; conversions across families (KG ↔ PCS) are item-specific and belong to Items (M2). | proposed |
| A-070 | **Numbering counters are per series and reset period** (`''`, the fiscal year code such as `FY2026/27`, or `2026-09`), advanced by `app.num_allocate` under a row lock inside the caller's transaction; every issued number is recorded append-only with its document, so duplicates are impossible at the database and the gapless audit is a query. A series starts every period at its `start_number`. | proposed |
| A-071 | **Series selection**: candidates are active series of the document type and company valid on the date whose branch (if set) matches; a branch-bound series scores 2, a fiscal-year-bound one 1; the highest score wins, then `is_default`, then code. A document may override to any applicable series. Templates use `{company} {branch} {yy} {yyyy} {mm} {fy}` and exactly one `{seq}`/`{seq:N}`; `{branch}` requires a branch-bound series. | proposed |
| A-073 | **Outbox ordering is strict per aggregate**: a message is claimed only when every earlier message of its aggregate is published, dead letters included, so a poison message halts its aggregate until an operator retries it; other aggregates continue. Handlers are idempotent through `ops.inbox` (row inserted in the handler's own transaction). Events with no registered handler are marked published immediately. | proposed |
| A-074 | **Jobs**: the final state is written in the job's own transaction (effect and completion commit together); a claim counts as an attempt, a graceful shutdown hands the job back without counting it, a lost heartbeat (2 minutes) requeues it, `JobFailedException` ends it as `failed` with no retry, other exceptions retry with backoff (1 s → 1 h) until `max_attempts` (default 5) and then `dead`. Job idempotency keys are unique per tenant and job type. | proposed |
| A-075 | **Schedules** use five-field cron in a named time zone; a new or re-enabled schedule is armed for its next slot without firing for the past; slots missed while no worker ran collapse into one job; one scheduler instance ticks at a time (advisory lock). Platform schedules (no tenant) are seeded: audit anchoring 02:00 UTC, verification 03:00 UTC, outbox archive 01:30 UTC, idempotency sweep hourly. | proposed |
| A-072 | **Permission keys are `module.entity.action`** (enforced by the catalogue), so ADR-0016's `numbering.reset` is `numbering.counter.reset`; period control keys live under `accounting.period.*` (`manage`, `reopen`, `post_in_soft_closed`) so the accountant template and the default SoD rule cover them. | proposed |
